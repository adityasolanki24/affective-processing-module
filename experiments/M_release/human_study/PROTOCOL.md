# APM v1.0 perception study (blinded clip package; protocol draft)

**Status: PENDING. Not run.**
- No participant has been recruited and no data have been collected.
- No ratings exist, and none may be generated by a model as a substitute.
- Until a study is run under ethics approval, every claim that people perceive APM's expression in a particular way
  remains untested.

This package extends programme H's H2 protocol (`experiments/H_expressive_intelligence/prereg/H2_PROTOCOL.md`) to
the v1.0 Isaac Sim footage. H2's ethics and authorization requirements apply unchanged.

## 1. Ethics and authorization (first)

- This is research with human participants (viewing videos, giving ratings), so it needs research-ethics approval
  or a formal institutional exemption **before any recruitment**. A researcher cannot self-certify an exemption.
- **Before data collection:**
  1. a written approval or exemption reference, recorded in `DEVIATIONS.md`;
  2. the user's explicit written go-ahead;
  3. the stimulus set frozen with SHA-256 hashes (`stimuli_manifest.json`);
  4. this protocol and its analysis plan committed.
- **Participants:**
  - adults with informed consent and the right to withdraw;
  - no identifiers stored with responses;
  - fair pay;
  - a debrief stating that the robots have no feelings and the rating words are experimental descriptors.

## 2. Questions

1. **History.** Can observers tell, better than chance, which of two clips of the same robot approaching the same
   person comes from a robot whose earlier encounters with that person involved being pushed? The clips are
   paired: same robot, same scene, same station, encounter after the history switch.
2. **Descriptors.** How do observers describe the approach (cautious, assertive, hesitant, relaxed, neutral / none)?
   Do the descriptors track the runtime's chosen intent (proximity, tempo, hesitation, OBSERVE/HOLD)?
3. **Generalisation.** Do 1 and 2 hold across the three bodies (NERVA, Booster T1, Nova Carter), which realise the
   same intent differently?

## 3. Stimuli (`make_stimuli.py`)

- **Source:** recorded Isaac Sim lifetimes (partition D, or X where noted), raw side-view footage only. There are
  no overlays, labels, beliefs or decisions, and the true type is never shown.
- **Pairs:** for each robot, 8 pairs. Each pair is the same scene and station and the first B1 approach after
  encounter 12:
  - "pushed history": change_B before the switch, or late_B after it;
  - "calm history": the safe-history counterpart.

  Selection follows a fixed rule (the first eligible encounter of indices 0–7), not how the clips look.
- **Clip:** from 1 s before the approach to 3 s after arrival, at most 30 s; 1280×720, 25 fps; audio none.
- **Blinding:** every clip gets a random 8-character ID. `stimuli_manifest.json` lists IDs and SHA-256 hashes
  only. The ID → condition key (`key.json`) is created separately, is not shipped to raters, and is opened only
  for analysis.

## 4. Procedure (online, within-subjects, about 20 minutes)

- **Training:** 2 neutral clips of each robot; no condition words.
- **Main block:** 24 pairs (8 per robot), with order and left/right position randomised per participant.
  1. **Forced choice:** "One of these robots had been pushed by this person in earlier meetings. Which one?"
     (left/right).
  2. **Descriptor** for each clip: cautious / assertive / hesitant / relaxed / neutral / none of these.
  3. **Confidence:** 1–5.
- **Attention checks:** 2 identical-clip pairs ("no difference" option shown only on these).

## 5. Analysis plan (frozen with the stimuli; `analyze.py` refuses to run without a real response file)

- **Q1.** Per robot, the proportion correct; a one-sided binomial test against 0.5 on participant-level means
  (mixed logistic model with participant and pair random intercepts as the primary analysis). Holm across robots.
- **Q2.** Descriptor distributions per runtime intent; Spearman correlation of "cautious" with chosen proximity
  (negative expected) and with OBSERVE/HOLD.
- **Q3.** Robot × condition interaction in the mixed model.
- **Exclusions:** failed attention checks; completion time under one third of the median.
- **Sample size:** 60 participants, planned. The power for a 62% correct rate (8 pairs per robot per participant)
  must be confirmed by simulation before the protocol is frozen; that has not been done.
