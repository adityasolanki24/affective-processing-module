# APM v1.0 release report

**Branch:** `release/apm-v1.0` (from programme L's `research/apm-v0.8-audit` at 84aeef9). Not merged.
Simulation only throughout: no hardware, no human participants.

**What is and is not claimed.**
- APM is a memory-and-decision runtime. Its beliefs are estimates of expected physical perturbation from specific
  people, not emotions. Nothing here supports claims about feelings or consciousness.
- People's interpretation of the robots' movement is untested. The perception study is prepared, not run.
- Safety results are simulation envelopes, not certification.

Protocol: [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) (committed before any V/X data). Deviations: [DEVIATIONS.md](DEVIATIONS.md).
Milestones and gate evidence: [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md).
